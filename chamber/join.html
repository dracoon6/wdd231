  • Member listing in Business Directory

  • Business resources and referral network

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Quarterly spotlight feature in directory

  • VIP access to networking events
  • Dedicated member services representative

  • Premium booth space at trade shows (4/year)
  • Complimentary marketing consultation
  • Board meeting invitation as guest speaker

Ideal for: Established businesses and corporations
